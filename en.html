Most people live here: not in crisis, but never calm. The engine still runs, slower and hotter, and you only notice when something smells burnt.

The problem in your brain
goes by many names.
It's the same handbrake.

This is how people describe it when they come to BrainBamboo. If you see yourself in more than one, this class is for you.

Burnout

You wake up already tired. What you used to enjoy now just weighs on you.

Mom brain

Ten tabs open in your head and none of them finishes loading.

Anxiety

A tight chest before anything happens. And afterward, it's still there.

Insomnia

A worn-out body and a racing mind, right when you turn off the light.

Irritability

You react before you think. Five minutes later, the guilt arrives.

Low mood

Nothing is really wrong, but nothing excites you. You function; you don't live.

These describe how people feel; they are not diagnoses. The class does not treat or diagnose any condition. It teaches you to recognize the pattern and release the handbrake.

What you'll walk away with

You leave the class knowing where your handbrake is
and with a way to release it that fits your day.

1

You see your pattern, mapped

The situation that sets it off, your body's first signal, and what you do next. Once you see it, it stops being automatic.

2

You learn a three-step pause

It fits in the middle of a meeting or a tantrum. No silence, no closed eyes, nobody notices.

3

You stop guessing whether it works

You get your score today and know how to repeat it in four weeks. What you don't measure, your brain normalizes.
